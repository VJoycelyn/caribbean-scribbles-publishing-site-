About Caribbean Scribbles

A digital publishing studio for Caribbean voices

Caribbean Scribbles Publishing blends island heritage with modern digital tools to help authors move from idea to ISBN—without losing their authentic voice.

Our mission

We exist to make it easier for Caribbean stories to reach readers everywhere: in classrooms, on tablets, in libraries, and in little hands at bedtime.

  • Champion books that center Caribbean children, families, culture, and everyday life.
  • Support new and emerging authors through practical, step-by-step guidance.
  • Use tech to keep publishing accessible, transparent, and sustainable.

What we focus on

We specialise in small-batch, high-care projects that reflect the region:

Children’s picture books Middle grade & chapter books Workbooks & activity books School & church resources Author coaching calls KDP & IngramSpark setup

For authors

How we can work together

Whether you’re working on your first children’s book or expanding a series, we’ll walk with you step by step.

1
Share your idea
Tell us about your story, who it’s for, and where you are in the writing process. We’ll review and suggest the best next steps.
2
Story & structure review
We help you strengthen your manuscript, clarify your message, and ensure your book works for your specific age group.
3
Design & publishing setup
We guide you through layout, file preparation, and platform setup (for example: Amazon KDP or other print-on-demand services).
4
Launch support
Get simple launch ideas tailored to your audience—school visits, community events, online promotions, or small local book runs.
